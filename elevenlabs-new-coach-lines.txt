ElevenLabs prompts for Zamaan's Daily Adventure - New Conversation Coach lines (November 2026)

These two lines are already wired into the app. Until the MP3 files exist, the coach
plays its normal travel line instead, so nothing breaks while you make them.

Voice direction (same as the other coach clips):
Warm, natural, upbeat, and relaxed. Speak like a friendly older cousin or coach. Keep it
clear and not too fast. Avoid sounding robotic, clinical, or overly childish. Leave a tiny
pause before the sentence he should repeat.

How to use:
1. In ElevenLabs, create each clip below with the same voice you used for the other coach clips.
2. Export each clip as MP3 and name it exactly as shown.
3. In GitHub, open the audio/personality folder, choose Add file > Upload files, and drop the two MP3s in.
