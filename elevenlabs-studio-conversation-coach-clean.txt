Zamaan's Daily Adventure - ElevenLabs Studio Pack
Conversation Coach / iMessage-style guided chat

HOW TO USE THIS FILE
1. In ElevenLabs Studio, use the voice direction below for the project/voice style.
2. Create each audio clip from the numbered prompt list.
3. Export each clip as MP3.
4. Rename each MP3 exactly as the filename shown.
5. Put the MP3 files in GitHub under: audio/personality/

VOICE DIRECTION
Warm, natural, clear, and encouraging. Sound like a friendly young coach who knows Zamaan well. Keep the pace calm and easy to follow. Add a little personality and confidence, but do not sound like a cartoon, a robot, a teacher giving a test, or a little-kid voice. Short pauses are okay. Make every line feel like a real person helping Zamaan practice conversation.

STYLE NOTES
- Speak directly to Zamaan.
- Keep it relaxed and upbeat.
- Use simple emotion: friendly, confident, patient.
- Do not overact.
- Do not make it sound clinical.
- Leave a tiny pause before the sentence he should repeat.
